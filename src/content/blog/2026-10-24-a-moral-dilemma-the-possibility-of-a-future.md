The pro life movement versus the pro choice movement both dabble in the arguments of whether or not terminating a pregnancy is acceptable. The pro life stance beliefs that life of a human begins at conception and therefore possesses humanity and the right to be alive. The pro choice stance supports body autonomy, but does not have a concrete stance on when life begins from a zygote, or an embryonic egg.

Politics will always be around to nip and scratch at the world, but abortion and birth both fall into ethical dilemmas rather than political. The taking of a life or the argument that there was no life to begin with has no place in a black tie with a briefcase. Therefore I will delve into this from a non-political point of view. An objective fact that nearly everyone in the world will agree on, is that murder is wrong, and abortion is murder, hence, it is wrong.

Now, awareness of difference of opinion has been acknowledged. It is understood that some believe that the body is ones own and should be able to use it how he or she likes. From a Catholic standpoint, this is incorrect, but it is also understood that many may not believe in His existence. So I will elaborate both perspectives. 

To start with the secular view of things, the National Library of Medicine published an article, "The Scientific Consensus on When a Human's Life Begins," and gave record of biologists from over a thousand academic institutions around the world complete studies on when human life begins. 96% of these biologists confirmed the result that human life begins at conception, or confirmed the fertilization view.
